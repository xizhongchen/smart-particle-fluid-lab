About Us关于我们

Pioneering Smart Particle Technologies引领智能颗粒技术前沿

The Smart Particle Fluid Lab (SPFL) is led by Prof. Xizhong Chen at the School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.智能颗粒流体实验室（SPFL）由上海交通大学化学化工学院陈锡忠长聘副教授领衔。

Our lab sits at the intersection of computational fluid dynamics, machine learning, and particle engineering. We develop multi-scale simulation methods and AI-driven tools to understand, predict, and optimize complex particle-fluid systems across chemical, pharmaceutical, and energy applications.实验室聚焦计算流体力学、机器学习与颗粒工程的交叉领域，开发多尺度模拟方法和AI驱动工具，用于理解、预测和优化化工、制药及能源领域的复杂颗粒流体系统。

With strong collaborations with world-leading institutions including the University of Cambridge, University of Edinburgh, and industry partners such as BASF, Pfizer, and Novartis, our research spans from fundamental science to real-world industrial impact.实验室与剑桥大学、爱丁堡大学等国际顶尖高校，以及BASF、Pfizer、Novartis等企业保持紧密合作，研究从基础科学延伸至工业应用。

Research研究方向

Our Research Areas研究领域

We combine physics-based modeling with data-driven AI to solve complex problems in particle-fluid systems我们将物理建模与数据驱动AI相结合，解决颗粒流体系统中的复杂问题

CFD-DEM SimulationCFD-DEM 模拟

High-fidelity computational fluid dynamics coupled with discrete element method for modeling gas-solid and liquid-solid multiphase flows at particle resolution.高保真计算流体力学耦合离散单元法，在颗粒分辨率下模拟气固和液固多相流。

CFD-DEM Multiphase Flow多相流 Granular Flow颗粒流

AI for Chemical EngineeringAI+化工

Machine learning, deep learning, and graph neural networks for surrogate modeling, inverse design, and real-time prediction of chemical processes.利用机器学习、深度学习和图神经网络进行化工过程的代理建模、逆向设计和实时预测。

GNN Deep Learning深度学习 Surrogate Model代理模型

Pharmaceutical Engineering制药工程

Multi-scale modeling of solid dosage forms including powder mixing, tablet compression, and dissolution prediction using physics-informed neural networks.固体制剂的多尺度建模，包括粉末混合、压片及利用物理信息神经网络进行溶出预测。

Solid Dosage固体制剂 Powder Mixing粉末混合 Dissolution溶出预测

Digital Twin数字孪生

Building digital twin systems for industrial equipment including fluidized bed jet mills, fixed-bed reactors, and corrosion pipeline monitoring.为流化床气流磨机、固定床反应器和腐蚀管道监测等工业设备构建数字孪生系统。

Real-time Prediction实时预测 Process Safety过程安全 Equipment Design设备设计

Particle Engineering颗粒工程

Fundamental study of particle breakage, adhesion, coating, and flowability using DEM bonded contact models and experimental characterization.利用DEM粘结接触模型和实验表征，研究颗粒破碎、粘附、涂层和流动性的基本规律。

Particle Breakage颗粒破碎 Adhesion粘附 Flowability流动性

Multi-scale Modeling多尺度模拟

Bridging nano, micro, and macro scales through dynamic multi-scale methods coupling two-fluid models with discrete particle models for industrial reactors.通过动态多尺度方法耦合双流体模型与离散颗粒模型，跨越纳米、微观和宏观尺度，服务于工业反应器。
