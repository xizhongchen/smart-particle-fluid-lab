About Us

Pioneering Smart Particle Technologies

The Smart Particle Fluid Lab (SPFL) is led by Prof. Xizhong Chen at the School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

Our lab sits at the intersection of computational fluid dynamics, machine learning, and particle engineering. We develop multi-scale simulation methods and AI-driven tools to understand, predict, and optimize complex particle-fluid systems across chemical, pharmaceutical, and energy applications.

With strong collaborations with world-leading institutions including the University of Cambridge, University of Edinburgh, and industry partners such as BASF, Pfizer, and Novartis, our research spans from fundamental science to real-world industrial impact.

Research

Our Research Areas

We combine physics-based modeling with data-driven AI to solve complex problems in particle-fluid systems

CFD-DEM Simulation

High-fidelity computational fluid dynamics coupled with discrete element method for modeling gas-solid and liquid-solid multiphase flows at particle resolution.

CFD-DEM Multiphase Flow Granular Flow

AI for Chemical Engineering

Machine learning, deep learning, and graph neural networks for surrogate modeling, inverse design, and real-time prediction of chemical processes.

GNN Deep Learning Surrogate Model

Pharmaceutical Engineering

Multi-scale modeling of solid dosage forms including powder mixing, tablet compression, and dissolution prediction using physics-informed neural networks.

Solid Dosage Powder Mixing Dissolution

Digital Twin

Building digital twin systems for industrial equipment including fluidized bed jet mills, fixed-bed reactors, and corrosion pipeline monitoring.

Real-time Prediction Process Safety Equipment Design

Particle Engineering

Fundamental study of particle breakage, adhesion, coating, and flowability using DEM bonded contact models and experimental characterization.

Particle Breakage Adhesion Flowability

Multi-scale Modeling

Bridging nano, micro, and macro scales through dynamic multi-scale methods coupling two-fluid models with discrete particle models for industrial reactors.

Coarse-Graining Population Balance Reduced-Order Model
